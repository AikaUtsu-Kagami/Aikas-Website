💗 | My Interests | 💗

| Gaming |

Gaming is one of my biggest hobbies. I enjoy a wide variety of games, especially ones with interesting stories, characters, worldbuilding and gameplay mechanics.
Some of my favourite franchises include Minecraft, Pokemon, Five Nights at Freddy's, The Legend of Zelda, Call of Duty, Watch Dogs and Cyberpunk 2077.
I also enjoy modding games, experimenting with different mechanics and creating my own environments.

| Programming & Technology |

I'm interested in programming, technology and learning how different systems work.
I currently enjoy working with HTML and CSS for web development, as well as Python for programming.
I'm also interested in cybersecurity, computer hardware, software development and operating systems.
One of my favourite fictional inspirations in this area is DedSec from Watch Dogs.

| Game Development |

Game development is something I've been interested in for a long time.
I enjoy the idea of creating entire worlds, designing gameplay mechanics and bringing stories to life through games.
I've experimented with Unreal Engine 5 and have worked on ideas for horror games and other projects.
I'm particularly interested in the combination of storytelling, level design and gameplay.

| Storytelling & Worldbuilding |

I enjoy writing stories, creating original characters and developing fictional worlds.
I also like exploring existing fictional universes, their lore, timelines and connections.
I find character development and complicated storylines particularly interesting.

| Anime & Animation |

I enjoy watching anime and animated series, especially those with interesting characters and relationships.

| Content Creation |

I enjoy streaming and creating gaming-related content.

| Art & Aesthetics |

I enjoy drawing, character design and exploring different visual styles.

| Music |
